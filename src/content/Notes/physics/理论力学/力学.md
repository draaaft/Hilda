# 经典力学笔记

## 直角坐标系

$$
\vec r=x\vec i+y\vec j+z\vec k
$$

$$
\frac{d\vec i}{dt}
=
\frac{d\vec j}{dt}
=
\frac{d\vec k}{dt}
=0
$$

$$
\vec v
=
\frac{d\vec r}{dt}
=
\frac{dx}{dt}\vec i
-
\frac{dy}{dt}\vec j
-
\frac{dz}{dt}\vec k
=
v_x\vec i+v_y\vec j+v_z\vec k
$$

$$
\vec a
=
\frac{d\vec v}{dt}
=
a_x\vec i+a_y\vec j+a_z\vec k
$$

牛二分量形式：

$$
F_x=m\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}
$$

$$
F_y=m\frac{d^2y}{dt^2}
$$

$$
F_z=m\frac{d^2z}{dt^2}
$$

## 平面极坐标系

$$
\vec e_r=\cos\theta\,\vec i+\sin\theta\,\vec j
$$

$$
\vec e_\theta=-\sin\theta\,\vec i+\cos\theta\,\vec j
$$

$$
\frac{d\vec e_r}{dt}
=
\frac{d\theta}{dt}\vec e_\theta
$$

$$
\frac{d\vec e_\theta}{dt}
=
-\frac{d\theta}{dt}\vec e_r
$$

> # 相对导数法则，只因数学容易遗失
>
> 对于我们想要的物理含义

$$
\vec r=r\vec e_r
$$

$$
\vec v
=
\frac{d\vec r}{dt}
=
\frac{dr}{dt}\vec e_r
+
r\frac{d\vec e_r}{dt}
=
\frac{dr}{dt}\vec e_r
+
r\frac{d\theta}{dt}\vec e_\theta
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec a
&=
\frac{d\vec v}{dt} \\
&=
\left[
\frac{d^2r}{dt^2}\vec e_r
-
\frac{dr}{dt}
\left(
\frac{d\vec e_r}{d\theta}
\right)
\frac{d\theta}{dt}
\right]
-
\left[
\frac{dr}{dt}\frac{d\theta}{dt}
\right]\vec e_\theta
-
r\left[
\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}\vec e_\theta
-
\frac{d\theta}{dt}
\frac{d\vec e_\theta}{d\theta}
\frac{d\theta}{dt}
\right] \\
&=
\left[
\frac{d^2r}{dt^2}
-r\left(\frac{d\theta}{dt}\right)^2
\right]\vec e_r
-
\left[
r\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}
-
2\frac{dr}{dt}\frac{d\theta}{dt}
\right]\vec e_\theta
\end{aligned}
$$

因此：

$$
\vec a_r
=
\frac{d^2r}{dt^2}
-r\left(\frac{d\theta}{dt}\right)^2
$$

$$
\vec a_\theta
=
r\left(\frac{d^2\theta}{dt^2}\right)
-
2\frac{dr}{dt}\frac{d\theta}{dt}
$$

## 自然坐标系

$$
\vec e_t=\text{切向}
$$

$$
\vec e_n=\text{法向}
$$

$$
\vec v=|v|\vec e_t
$$

$$
\vec a
=
\frac{d\vec v}{dt}
=
\frac{dv}{dt}\vec e_t
-
v\frac{d\vec e_t}{dt}
$$

$$
\frac{d\vec e_t}{dt}
=
\frac{v}{\rho}\vec e_n
$$

因此：

$$
\vec a
=
\frac{dv}{dt}\vec e_t
-
\frac{v^2}{\rho}\vec e_n
$$

$$
F_t
=
m\frac{dv}{dt}\vec e_t
$$

$$
F_n
=
m\frac{v^2}{\rho}\vec e_n
$$

### 曲率半径

$$
ds=\rho\,d\theta
$$

（$\rho$ 曲率半径）

$$
d\vec e_t
=
1\cdot d\theta
=
d\theta
$$

因此：

$$
d\vec e_t
=
\frac{1}{\rho}ds
$$

## 柱坐标系

$$
\vec r
=
\rho\vec e_\rho+z\vec k
$$

$$
\frac{d\vec k}{dt}=0
$$

$$
d\vec e_\rho=d\varphi\vec e_\varphi
$$

$$
d\vec e_\varphi=-d\varphi\vec e_\rho
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v
&=
\frac{d\vec r}{dt} \\
&=
\dot\rho\vec e_\rho
-
\rho\frac{d\vec e_\rho}{dt}
-
\frac{dz}{dt}\vec k
+0
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
a_\rho
=
\frac{d^2\rho}{dt^2}
-
\rho\left(\frac{d\varphi}{dt}\right)^2
$$

$$
a_\varphi
=
\rho\frac{d^2\varphi}{dt^2}
-
2\frac{d\rho}{dt}\frac{d\varphi}{dt}
$$

$$
a_z
=
\frac{d^2z}{dt^2}
$$

## 球坐标系

$$
\vec e_r
=
\sin\theta\cos\varphi\,\vec i
-
\sin\theta\sin\varphi\,\vec j
-
\cos\theta\,\vec k
$$

$$
\vec e_\theta
=
\cos\theta\cos\varphi\,\vec i
-
\cos\theta\sin\varphi\,\vec j
+
\sin\theta\,\vec k
$$

$$
\vec e_\varphi
=
-\sin\varphi\,\vec i
-
\cos\varphi\,\vec j
$$

$$
d\vec e_r
=
d\theta\,\vec e_\theta
-
\sin\theta\,d\varphi\,\vec e_\varphi
$$

$$
d\vec e_\theta
=
-d\theta\,\vec e_r
-
\cos\theta\,d\varphi\,\vec e_\varphi
$$

$$
d\vec e_\varphi
=
-\sin\theta\,d\varphi\,\vec e_r
-
\cos\theta\,d\varphi\,\vec e_\theta
$$

$$
\vec r=r\vec e_r
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec v
&=
\frac{d}{dt}(r\vec e_r)\\
&=
\dot r\vec e_r
-
r\left(
\dot\theta\vec e_\theta
-
\sin\theta\dot\varphi\vec e_\varphi
\right)\\
&=
\dot r\vec e_r
-
r\dot\theta\vec e_\theta
-
r\sin\theta\dot\varphi\vec e_\varphi
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec a
&=
\frac{d\vec v}{dt}\\
&=
\ddot r\vec e_r
-
\dot r\vec e_r^{\,\prime}
-
r\dot\theta\vec e_\theta
-
r\ddot\theta\vec e_\theta
-
r\dot\theta\vec e_\theta^{\,\prime}
-
r\sin\theta\dot\varphi\vec e_\varphi^{\,\prime}\\
&\quad
-
r\cos\theta\dot\theta\dot\varphi\vec e_\varphi
-
r\sin\theta\ddot\varphi\vec e_\varphi
-
r\sin\theta\dot\varphi\vec e_\varphi^{\,\prime}
\end{aligned}
$$

整理：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\vec a
={}&
\vec e_r
\left(
\ddot r
-r\dot\theta^2
-r\sin^2\theta\dot\varphi^2
\right)\\
&+
\vec e_\theta
\left(
2\dot r\dot\theta
+r\ddot\theta
-r\sin\theta\cos\theta\dot\varphi^2
\right)\\
&+
\vec e_\varphi
\left(
2\sin\theta\dot r\dot\varphi
-
2r\cos\theta\dot\theta\dot\varphi
-
r\sin\theta\ddot\varphi
\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

其中：

$$
\vec a_r
=
\ddot r
-r\dot\theta^2
-r\sin^2\theta\dot\varphi^2
$$

$$
\vec a_\theta
=
2\dot r\dot\theta
+r\ddot\theta
-r\sin\theta\cos\theta\dot\varphi^2
$$

$$
\vec a_\varphi
=
2\sin\theta\dot r\dot\varphi
-
2\cos\theta\dot\varphi\dot\theta
-
r\sin\theta\ddot\varphi
$$

$$
\vec F_r=m\vec a_r
$$

$$
\vec F_\theta=m\vec a_\theta
$$

$$
\vec F_\varphi=m\vec a_\varphi
$$

$$
\vec F=m\vec a
$$

# 质点系

## 内力满足的条件

### 弱形式

$$
\vec F_{ij}=-\vec F_{ji}
$$

$$
\Rightarrow
\text{内力矢量和为零}
$$

（力大小等，方向反）

### 强形式

$$
\vec F_{ij}\parallel\vec F_{ji}
\parallel
(\vec r_i-\vec r_j)
$$

$$
\Rightarrow
\text{内力对任意一点合力矩也为零}
$$

即使大反作用定理成立，在同一直线上：

（两质点连线）

## 质点系关于一点 P 的角动量定理——矢量形式

质点系关于点 P 的角动量：

$$
\vec L_P
=
\sum_i
\vec r_{iP}\times m_i\vec v_i
$$

其中 $\vec v_i$ 相对于惯性坐标系而非 P 点。

$$
\frac{d\vec L_P}{dt}
=
\sum_i
\frac{d\vec r_{iP}}{dt}
\times
m_i\vec v_i
-
\sum_i
\vec r_{iP}\times m_i\vec a_i
$$

因为：

$$
\frac{d\vec r_{iP}}{dt}
=
\vec v_i-\vec v_P
$$

所以：

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{d\vec L_P}{dt}
&=
\sum_i
(\vec v_i-\vec v_P)
\times m_i\vec v_i
-
\sum_i
\vec r_{iP}\times m_i\vec a_i
\\
&=
-\vec v_P\times
\sum_i m_i\vec v_i
-
\sum_i
\vec r_{iP}\times m_i\vec a_i
\\
&=
-\vec v_P\times\vec P
-
\sum_i
\vec r_{iP}\times m_i\vec a_i
\end{aligned}
$$

若内力矩为零（即力等大反向且作用点连线）：

$$
\frac{d\vec L_P}{dt}
=
\vec P\times\vec v_P
-
\vec M_P^{\,ext}
$$
